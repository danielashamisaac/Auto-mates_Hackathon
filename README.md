**Atuto-mate**: is a student registration, payment and record management system with three major functionalities:
1. **student capturing:** consisting of five major steps a. Catigory Selection b. Department Selection c. Eligibility Test D. Bio Collection e. Payment (Through a paystack: Test mode Payment gatway) 
2. **Payment portal:** Verify payment through a refrence ID and redowloading of recipte and timetable
3. **Admin portal:** Viewing KPI summary of students overall registrations record, adjusting department slots as well as downloading full detail records into his local device to avoid manual record taking

 Our major Objective is to unify student fragmented registration process as well as automate Student record Keeping
